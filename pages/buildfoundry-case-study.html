The Vision

Build Foundry is designed to be the single platform where everything Nick builds lives — a professional service hub that brings web development, branding, and digital operations together under one roof.

Rather than scattered freelance work and informal outreach, Build Foundry centralizes client intake, project delivery, and service management into a polished, scalable product that grows with the business.

Planned Features

Build Foundry is being scoped to handle every touchpoint of a client engagement — from first contact to final delivery.

🖥️
Client Portal
Dedicated login for clients to track project progress, view deliverables, approve work, and communicate directly.
🎨
Branding Suite
Structured intake for branding projects — logos, identity systems, and marketing assets — with asset delivery built in.
⚙️
Scheduling Automation
Automated discovery calls, project kickoffs, and check-in scheduling — no manual back-and-forth.
🔗
CRM Integrations
Native connection to tools like Stripe for payments, and a lightweight built-in CRM for managing client relationships and follow-ups.
