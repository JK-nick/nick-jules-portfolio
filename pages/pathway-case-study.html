The Problem

College academic planning is fragmented, confusing, and often left entirely to the student. Course catalogs are dense, advisor appointments are scarce, and there's no tool that gives students a clear, personalized picture of what their next four years actually look like.

Pathway is being built to solve that — one institution, one major, one student at a time. Not a generic planner, but an intelligent system that actually knows your curriculum.

The Solution

Pathway ingests a school's course catalog and degree requirements, then generates a semester-by-semester 4-year plan tailored to the student's major, preferences, and credit history.

Layered on top is an AI advisor — a conversational interface that answers questions, suggests adjustments, and keeps the student on track throughout their degree.

Core Features

Pathway is being built around four pillars: intelligent planning, real-time guidance, institutional integration, and student ownership.

🗓️
4-Year Degree Planner
Auto-generated semester plans based on major requirements, prerequisites, and student credit history.
🤖
AI Academic Advisor
Conversational AI that answers questions about courses, requirements, and adjustments — available 24/7.
🏫
Institutional Integration
Pulls directly from school course catalogs and degree audits rather than relying on generic templates.
📊
Progress Tracking
Visual credit tracking, graduation requirement checks, and semester load balancing built into the dashboard.
