The Olivia

A luxury hair booking platform that simplifies online scheduling, payments, and client management for beauty professionals and their clients.

Live Site GitHub
+45% Bookings

The Problem

Booking appointments manually was time-consuming and inefficient for both clients and stylists. The client wanted a modern, automated solution that would increase bookings and improve customer experience.

Without a dedicated platform, potential clients had no easy way to browse services, check availability, or secure appointments — leading to missed revenue and inconsistent scheduling.

The Solution

I built a responsive booking platform with an elegant UI, online scheduling, payment integration, and an admin dashboard to manage appointments and clients seamlessly.

The site features a full service menu with pricing, a custom booking form with deposit handling, a policies page, and an about section — all designed to build trust and convert visitors into clients.

Operational Features

Beyond the frontend, this project required building a complete client operations system — not just a website, but a workflow that runs the business end-to-end.

📅
Appointment Scheduling
Integrated automated booking workflows and client scheduling systems to eliminate manual back-and-forth and allow clients to self-book in real time.
🔔
Automated Reminders
Configured automated confirmation and reminder flows to reduce no-shows and keep clients informed throughout the appointment lifecycle.
📋
Client Intake Workflows
Built structured intake forms capturing service preferences, deposit acknowledgment, and policy sign-off — standardizing every new client interaction.
💬
Client Communication Systems
Established end-to-end communication pipelines including booking confirmations, deposit instructions, and follow-up touchpoints — all automated.

Challenges

  • Building a smooth, multi-step booking flow that felt intuitive without overwhelming first-time clients
  • Designing a deposit system that clearly communicated payment expectations and built client trust
  • Creating a service menu easy to read across mobile and desktop with varying service tiers and pricing
  • Optimizing for mobile performance since the majority of traffic comes from Instagram and social referrals

Results

  • +45% increase in bookings within the first month
  • Reduced no-shows with automated deposit requirements
  • Improved client satisfaction and professional perception
  • Stylist now manages all appointments from a single platform
